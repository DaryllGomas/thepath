## Notes
- All Sonniss files were fetched from the gamesounds.xyz mirror of the official Sonniss.com GDC Game Audio Bundles (free, no login; browser User-Agent header sent per site convention). Sonniss states these bundles are royalty-free for any commercial or non-commercial project, no attribution required, forever.
- Pixabay downloads required no account/login; the "Free download" button on both pages is anonymously accessible. Pixabay's Content License permits free commercial/non-commercial use; the two files here originate from Freesound contributors and Pixabay's own guidance is to credit the artist when possible, so attribution lines are included above even though not strictly required.
- Freesound.org itself was not used directly (its originals require OAuth login per the brief) — the two Freesound-sourced clips were obtained through Pixabay's mirror, which serves full-quality files without any login.
- BBC Sound Effects was not used (personal/educational licence only, excluded per the brief).
- No sound in this folder was synthesized; every file traces back to a microphone recording of real equipment (analogue/vintage telephones, a real modem call, a real CRT monitor, a real 5.25" floppy drive, real typewriter/keyboard keys, and real TV static).
